    Welcome! 👋

    1. These 4 little people are your AI workers. They really work inside Antigravity.
    2. Press Watch delegation to see them walk and talk. This part is just animation.
    3. To give real work, click a worker, type a task and press Send task.

    Walking and meetings are cartoons. Tasks and logs are real.

    Simple guide

    AI worker = an agent you created in Antigravity (like QA or Frontend).

    Watch delegation = a test animation: the boss walks to a colleague's desk.

    Call a meeting = everyone gathers together for a moment.

    Send task = real work. The agent will use your AI quota and can edit files.

    What's happening = live messages from your agents, copied from Antigravity.

    Layout and Settings are advanced controls from the original canvas. They are hidden here because you do not need them to give tasks.

    Everything you need is right here on this screen.
